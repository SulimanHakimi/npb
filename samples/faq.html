Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about short links, analytics and your account.

Getting started

What is Bitlinks?

Bitlinks turns long web addresses into short, branded links and shows you who clicks them.

Do I need an account to make a short link?

You can make a few links without an account. Sign up for free to keep them and see their clicks.

Links and analytics

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. On the Pro plan you can connect a domain you own, for example go.yourcompany.com.

What does the analytics page show?

Clicks over time, the countries and cities they came from, and the devices and browsers people used.

Do short links ever expire?

No. Your links keep working unless you delete them or set an end date yourself.

Billing

How much does Pro cost?

Pro is $12 a month, or $120 a year if you pay yearly. You can cancel at any time.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and bank transfer for yearly plans.
